## Overview
Stranded was built as a part of my capstone course for my Computer Science degree at the University of Michigan. This was the culmination of a semester long class on game design that was a rapid crash course in how to build video games from scratch. We used the Unity game engine to build stranded and all programming was done in C#.

## Game Description
Stranded is a 3D first person Action-Adventure RPG  Survival game. After being stranded on a foreign planet the player is forced to gather parts from across the world to repair their ship and blast off! But be careful, there may be monsters lurking in the shadows.
